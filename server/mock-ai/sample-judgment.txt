IN THE HIGH COURT OF KARNATAKA AT BENGALURU

Writ Petition No. 1842 of 2024 (GM-RES)

Anandi Devi and Others
... Petitioners

Versus

State of Karnataka through Principal Secretary, Revenue Department and Others
... Respondents

Date of Order: 14 February 2024

Coram: Hon'ble Justice R. Narayan

JUDGMENT

1. The petitioners have approached this Court seeking implementation of the rehabilitation package notified for displaced families affected by the Amaravathi Reservoir Expansion Project.

2. The record indicates that compensation was sanctioned in 2022 but remained unpaid to several eligible families. The District Administration has also not completed the mutation of alternative land parcels allotted under the rehabilitation policy.

3. The State submits that inter-departmental reconciliation is pending between the Revenue Department, Finance Department and Public Works Department. The explanation is not acceptable when the rights of displaced citizens stand crystallised by the government notification.

4. The Police Department has reported continuing obstruction at the resettlement site. The Court expects preventive measures so that lawful rehabilitation is not frustrated.

5. The Legal Department is directed to examine whether an intra-court appeal is necessary on the narrow question of interest liability, without delaying compliance with the operative directions below.

OPERATIVE DIRECTIONS

6. The Revenue Department shall, within 30 days from the date of this order, complete verification of the list of eligible displaced families and publish the final beneficiary register.

7. The Finance Department shall release the sanctioned compensation amount to all verified beneficiaries within 45 days from the date of receipt of the final beneficiary register.

8. The District Administration shall complete mutation and handover of alternative land parcels within 60 days and file a compliance affidavit before the Registrar Judicial.

9. The Police Department shall provide necessary protection at the resettlement site until handover is completed and shall submit a weekly status report to the Deputy Commissioner.

10. The Public Works Department shall review the condition of access roads, drinking water points and temporary shelters and submit a remediation plan within 21 days.

11. The Legal Department may review the issue of interest liability and, if so advised, file an appeal within the period of limitation. Such review shall not operate as a stay of compliance.

12. Non-compliance with these directions may invite proceedings under the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971. The writ petition is disposed of.
